Functions of Density

Hi! I'm Peter and this is my portfolio/blog website! I'm mainly interested in exploring cities and city policy through a quantitative lens (the functions associated with density!). The work on this website explores that area, although it also includes more general quantitative work from my school projects as well as non-policy-related passion projects.
