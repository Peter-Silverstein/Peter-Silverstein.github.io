Functions of Density

A blog/portfolio for Peter Silverstein, focusing on better understanding people in cities through quantitative methods (the functions associated with density!). The work on this website explores the sustainable future of urban policy--what solutions are best for people? Most effective for the cost? Which approaches work and which don't?
